# Substantive Review Template

Use this file to define the domain-specific review criteria for this project.
This replaces hidden project-local reviewer agents with an explicit local rubric.

## Core Questions
- What is the main contribution?
- What claims require the strongest evidence?
- What kinds of overclaiming are most likely in this project?
- What assumptions or design choices must always be checked?

## Contribution And Positioning
- What literature or debates should Codex keep in view?
- What kinds of novelty claims require caution?

## Identification And Methodology
- What identifying assumptions matter most?
- What robustness or sensitivity checks are expected?
- What kinds of causal language are too strong for this project?

## Data And Measurement
- Which datasets, variables, or coding decisions are easiest to misstate?
- Which definitions must remain consistent across paper, code, and slides?

## Replication And Reporting
- Which tables and figures are central?
- What reproducibility standards must be met before calling the project ready?
